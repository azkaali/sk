1. Introduction
1.1 Purpose
This document defines the functional and non-functional requirements for SkillConnect, a cross-platform mobile application that connects everyday users who need work done ("Customers") with independent skilled laborers and tradespeople ("Providers") â€” e.g. electricians, plumbers, carpenters, painters, mechanics, movers, cleaners, masons, welders, AC technicians, and similar trades. The platform operates on a location-aware, on-demand model similar in interaction pattern to ride-hailing apps, and uses an open, negotiable bidding mechanism similar to InDrive rather than fixed platform-set pricing.
1.2 Scope
The system consists of three client applications sharing one backend:
Customer App (iOS & Android) â€” post job requests, discover nearby providers, receive and compare bids, chat, track provider arrival, pay, and rate.
Provider App (iOS & Android) â€” set availability and service radius, view nearby job requests, submit bids, navigate to job location, chat, receive payment, and build a rating profile.
Admin Web Console â€” user/provider verification (KYC), dispute resolution, category & pricing-guardrail management, analytics, and content moderation.
Out of scope for v1.0: full-time employment/staffing contracts, insurance underwriting, and in-app manufacturing/e-commerce of materials (may be considered for a later phase).

2. Overall Description
2.1 Product Perspective
SkillConnect is a new, standalone two-sided marketplace product. It is architected as a single backend (API + real-time services) with two native-feeling mobile front ends built from one shared cross-platform codebase, plus a web-based admin console.
2.2 Product Functions (Summary)
Account creation and role selection (Customer / Provider), with Provider identity and skill verification.
Location-based discovery: Customers see nearby available Providers by trade category; Providers see nearby open Job Requests.
Job posting with description, category, photos, preferred schedule, and budget range.
Open bidding: multiple Providers can submit competing bids on a Job Request; Customer compares and selects.
In-app chat and voice call masking (privacy-preserving) between matched Customer and Provider.
Live location tracking of the Provider en route, similar to ride-hailing trip tracking.
In-app payments (cash-on-completion option and digital payment), platform commission handling, and payouts to Providers.
Two-way rating and review system after job completion.
Push notifications for bid activity, matches, arrival, and payment events.
Admin tools for verification, dispute handling, and marketplace health monitoring.

2.4 Operating Environment
Mobile OS: iOS 16+ and Android 10+ (API level 29+).
Backend: cloud-hosted (AWS/GCP/Azure â€” see Section 7), horizontally scalable.
Network: must degrade gracefully on 3G/4G and intermittent connectivity, common in field/on-site conditions.
2.5 Design and Implementation Constraints
Must comply with app store review guidelines for two-sided gig-work marketplaces (Apple App Store & Google Play).
Must comply with local labor/trade licensing rules in each launch market; Provider onboarding must capture required license/ID fields per region.
Payment flows must go through a PCI-DSS compliant processor; the app itself must not store raw card data.
Data protection: personal data handling must comply with applicable regional privacy law (e.g., GDPR-equivalent where relevant).
2.6 Assumptions and Dependencies
Users have GPS-capable smartphones and grant location permission for core functionality to work.
A third-party maps/geolocation provider and a payment gateway are available and contracted.
SMS/OTP delivery provider is available in all launch regions.
3. Functional Requirements
3.1 Onboarding, Authentication & Verification
FR-1.1  The system shall allow sign-up via phone number + OTP, with optional email and social login (Google/Apple).
FR-1.2  The system shall require the user to select a role: Customer, Provider, or both.
FR-1.3  The system shall require Providers to complete a verification flow: government ID upload, live selfie match, trade/skill category selection, optional certifications/licenses, and years of experience.
FR-1.4  The system shall place newly registered Providers into a "Pending Verification" state; they shall not appear in Customer search results or be able to bid until Admin approves them.
FR-1.5  The system shall allow Customers to use core browsing features as guests but require authentication before posting a job or contacting a Provider.
FR-1.6  The system shall support profile management: name, photo, address book of saved locations, and (for Providers) service categories, service radius, and working hours.
3.2 Location & Discovery (Map View)
FR-2.1  The system shall display a map-based home screen (ride-hailing style) showing the user's current location and nearby relevant markers: available Providers (for Customers) or open Job Requests (for Providers).
FR-2.2  The system shall let Customers filter Providers by trade category, minimum rating, verification badge, and distance.
FR-2.3  The system shall let Providers set a configurable service radius and toggle an Online/Offline availability status, mirroring driver availability toggles in ride-hailing apps.
FR-2.4  The system shall update Provider location in near real time (target: every 5â€“10 seconds while online) for accurate map display.
FR-2.5  The system shall support search-by-address in addition to current GPS location, so Customers can request work at a different location (e.g., a rental property).
3.3 Job Posting
FR-3.1  The system shall allow a Customer to create a Job Request specifying: category, free-text description, up to 5 photos, desired schedule (Now / Scheduled date-time), job location, and an optional target budget range.
FR-3.2  The system shall allow a Customer to mark a Job Request as "Open to all nearby Providers" or "Invite specific Providers" (e.g., previously used, favorited providers).
FR-3.3  The system shall broadcast new Open Job Requests to eligible, online, in-radius Providers matching the selected category.
FR-3.4  The system shall allow a Customer to edit or cancel a Job Request prior to accepting a bid, with cancellation reason capture.
3.4 Bidding & Matching (InDrive-style)
FR-4.1  The system shall allow any eligible Provider to submit a bid on an open Job Request, consisting of a proposed price, an ETA, and an optional message.
FR-4.2  The system shall allow multiple Providers to bid concurrently on the same Job Request until the Customer accepts one.
FR-4.3  The system shall allow the Customer to counter-offer a submitted bid (propose a different price back to the Provider), enabling a negotiation loop consistent with the InDrive bidding model.
FR-4.4  The system shall allow a Provider to revise or withdraw a bid before it is accepted.
FR-4.5  The system shall present all received bids to the Customer sorted by price, rating, and ETA, and shall show each bidding Provider's profile, rating, and verification badge.
FR-4.6  Upon Customer acceptance of a single bid, the system shall automatically close/expire all other bids on that Job Request and notify the non-selected Providers.
FR-4.7  The system shall enforce a configurable minimum and maximum bid guardrail per category (set by Admin) to curb spam or predatory pricing, while otherwise leaving price negotiation open between the two parties.
FR-4.8  The system shall support a bid expiry window (e.g., 10â€“15 minutes) after which an un-actioned bid automatically closes.
3.5 In-App Communication
FR-5.1  The system shall provide in-app text chat between a Customer and a Provider, enabled once a bid negotiation begins.
FR-5.2  The system shall provide masked (number-privacy-preserving) voice calling between matched parties.
FR-5.3  The system shall retain chat history for the duration of the job plus a configurable retention period for dispute-resolution purposes.
3.6 Job Execution & Tracking
FR-6.1  Once a bid is accepted, the system shall create a Job record with status states: Accepted â†’ Provider En Route â†’ Arrived â†’ In Progress â†’ Completed â†’ (optionally) Disputed/Cancelled.
FR-6.2  The system shall show the Customer live map tracking of the en-route Provider with ETA, mirroring ride-hailing trip tracking.
FR-6.3  The system shall require the Provider to mark "Arrived" and "Job Started"/"Job Completed" from the app, timestamping each transition.
FR-6.4  The system shall allow either party to raise a dispute on a Job (e.g., no-show, scope disagreement, quality issue), routing it to Admin.
FR-6.5  The system shall support before/after photo capture at job completion for quality and dispute evidence.
3.7 Payments
FR-7.1  The system shall support in-app digital payment (card/wallet) and a cash-on-completion option, both recorded against the Job.
FR-7.2  The system shall calculate and deduct a platform commission from the agreed job price for digital payments, and reconcile commission owed for cash jobs against the Provider's account.
FR-7.3  The system shall support scheduled payouts to Provider bank accounts / mobile wallets, with a payout history view.
FR-7.4  The system shall generate a digital receipt/invoice for each completed Job.
FR-7.5  The system shall support refunds/adjustments initiated by Admin as part of dispute resolution.
3.8 Ratings, Reviews & Trust
FR-8.1  The system shall prompt both Customer and Provider to rate each other (1â€“5 stars) and leave an optional written review after Job Completion.
FR-8.2  The system shall display a Provider's aggregate rating, completed-job count, response rate, and verification badges on their profile and in bid lists.
FR-8.3  The system shall allow Admin to suspend or deactivate accounts that fall below a configurable rating/dispute threshold.
3.9 Notifications
FR-9.1  The system shall send push notifications for: new job broadcast (to Providers), new bid received (to Customers), bid accepted/declined, chat messages, Provider en route/arrived, payment confirmation, and rating prompts.
FR-9.2  The system shall allow users to configure notification preferences per category.
3.10 Admin Console
FR-10.1  The system shall provide Admin tools to review and approve/reject Provider verification submissions.
FR-10.2  The system shall provide Admin tools to configure service categories, min/max bid guardrails per category, and platform commission rates.
FR-10.3  The system shall provide a dispute-resolution queue with case detail (chat log, photos, job timeline) and resolution actions (refund, payout release, account action).
FR-10.4  The system shall provide analytics dashboards: active users, job volume, average bid-to-accept time, GMV, cancellation rate, and category-level supply/demand balance.

6. External Interface Requirements
6.1 Third-Party Integrations
Maps & Geolocation API (e.g., Google Maps Platform) â€” map rendering, geocoding, distance/ETA calculation.
Push Notification service (Firebase Cloud Messaging for Android, Apple Push Notification service for iOS).
SMS/OTP provider for phone verification.

6.2 Internal APIs
A REST/GraphQL API layer plus a real-time channel (WebSocket or equivalent, e.g., for live location and bid updates) shall be exposed to both mobile clients and the Admin console.
7. Recommended Technology Approach
7.1 Mobile Client
A single cross-platform codebase is recommended so one team can ship both iOS and Android from shared logic:
React Native (with TypeScript) â€” large ecosystem, strong maps/payment/library support, or
Flutter (Dart) â€” strong performance and consistent UI rendering across platforms.
Either is suitable; the choice typically comes down to team familiarity. Native modules will still be needed for background location tracking and payment SDKs on both platforms.
7.2 Backend
API layer: Node.js (NestJS/Express) or a Go/Java service, exposing REST/GraphQL.
Real-time layer: WebSocket service (e.g., Socket.io) or a managed real-time database for live location/bid updates.
Database: PostgreSQL (with PostGIS extension for geospatial queries) for core relational data; Redis for caching and presence (online/offline) state.
Cloud hosting: AWS, GCP, or Azure, containerized (Docker/Kubernetes) for horizontal scaling.
7.3 High-Level Architecture
Customer App and Provider App (shared cross-platform codebase, role-based UI) communicate with an API Gateway, which routes to: Auth Service, Job/Bid Service, Location Service (geospatial matching), Payment Service, Notification Service, and Chat Service. A real-time channel pushes location updates and bid events directly to connected clients. The Admin Console consumes the same API Gateway with elevated permissions.
8. Assumptions, Constraints & Risks
8.1 Assumptions
Initial launch targets a single city/region to validate supply/demand balance before multi-region expansion.
Providers are independent contractors, not employees, of the platform (classification to be confirmed with local legal counsel).
8.2 Constraints
Regulatory: gig-worker classification and trade licensing rules vary by region and may require region-specific onboarding fields.
Trust & safety: unlike ride-hailing, jobs often happen inside a Customer's home, raising the bar for identity verification and in-app safety features (e.g., share job status with a contact, SOS button).
8.3 Key Risks
Cold-start / liquidity risk: insufficient Providers in a new area leads to poor Customer experience â€” mitigate with a manual/seeded Provider onboarding push at launch.
Race-to-the-bottom pricing from open bidding â€” mitigate with the min/max bid guardrails (FR-4.7) and rating-weighted bid display.
Fraud/no-show risk â€” mitigate with verification, ratings, and a cancellation/no-show penalty policy.
9. Success Metrics & Future Scope
9.1 Success Metrics (v1.0)
Average time from Job Request posted to first bid received.
Bid-to-accept conversion rate.
Job completion rate vs. cancellation/dispute rate.
Provider retention (7-day / 30-day active) and Customer repeat-booking rate.
9.2 Future Scope (Post-v1.0)

Subscription/membership tier for frequent Customers (e.g., recurring cleaning).
Team/crew bidding for larger jobs (multiple Providers on one Job Request).
In-app insurance or guarantee add-on for higher-value jobs.
Provider loyalty/incentive program based on rating and volume.
